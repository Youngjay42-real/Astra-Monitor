Earth color map and static fallback: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, Blue Marble (2004), https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915/ . Surface composite, not a live satellite feed.

Night lights and cloud maps: Three.js example planet textures, https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures/planets .

The globe is an interactive visualization with illustrative lighting and accelerated rotation. It does not represent current Earth illumination or real-time mission tracking.
